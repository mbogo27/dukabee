Grade A is the only grade we sell. Every laptop is inspected before it is listed, and a unit that fails inspection is rejected, not listed at a lower grade.

## What we check

- **Cosmetics:** the body, lid, hinges and palm rest.
- **Battery health:** how much charge the battery still holds compared with when it was new.
- **Screen:** dead pixels, pressure marks and backlight.
- **Keyboard and touchpad:** every key and the touchpad.
- **BIOS and password locks:** no BIOS password and no company management lock, so the laptop is fully yours.

The exact pass criteria for each check, including the minimum battery health, are being finalised with our supplier and will be published on this page.
