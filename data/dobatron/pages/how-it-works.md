We source laptops through our supplier network rather than holding stock, so every order starts with a quick check on WhatsApp.

## Four steps

- **Pick a laptop.** Browse [laptops](/laptops), read a [guide](/guides), or let [Find my laptop](/find-my-laptop) suggest two or three.
- **Confirm on WhatsApp.** Tap the WhatsApp button on any laptop, or check out from your cart. We confirm the exact unit is available and send photos, a video and its battery health.
- **Inspect it.** Check the laptop at pickup or delivery. [Our Grade A page](/grade-a) and [budget guide](/guides/best-refurbished-laptops-kenya-by-budget) list what to look at.
- **Then pay.** We accept M-Pesa.

## Delivery and pickup

We agree delivery or a pickup point with you on WhatsApp for each order.

## Warranty and returns

Warranty and returns terms are being finalised. Ask us on WhatsApp before you buy and we'll tell you what applies to your unit.
