Dobatron is a laptop plug. We don't keep a warehouse of laptops: we source Grade A refurbished laptops through our supplier network, check each unit, and confirm it with you on WhatsApp before you pay.

## Who is behind it

- **Mbogo**, marketing and tech: builds this site and helps you find the right laptop.
- **Francis**, sourcing, fulfilment and quality assurance: sources and tests every unit before it is listed.

## How it works

- **Pick a laptop** on the site, or [let us suggest one](/find-my-laptop).
- **Confirm on WhatsApp.** We send photos, a video and the battery health of the exact unit.
- **Inspect it** at pickup or delivery.
- **Then pay.**

More detail on [how buying from us works](/how-it-works) and on [what Grade A means](/grade-a).
