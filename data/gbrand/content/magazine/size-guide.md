Most exchanges we handle come down to one thing: size. The good news is that two minutes with a tape measure gets it right the first time.

## Measure your chest

Wrap a tape measure around the fullest part of your chest, just under your armpits. Keep it level and snug, not tight. Then match the number to the table below.

| Size | Chest (cm) | Length (cm) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| S | 88–96 | 70 |
| M | 96–104 | 72 |
| L | 104–112 | 74 |
| XL | 112–120 | 76 |
| XXL | 120–128 | 78 |

## Between two sizes?

Go for the bigger one. Jerseys are made to move, and a slightly looser fit looks better over a T-shirt or under a jacket.

## Want it fitted or relaxed?

- **Fitted:** pick the size your chest measurement lands on.
- **Relaxed or streetwear:** go one size up.
- **Buying a gift:** check a T-shirt they already wear and compare chest width.
