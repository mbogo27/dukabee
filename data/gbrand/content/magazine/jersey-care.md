A jersey that's washed right keeps its colour, its crest and its print for seasons. Here's the routine we recommend.

1. **Turn it inside out.** This protects the badge, sponsor print and any name and number.
2. **Wash cold.** Hand wash or a gentle machine cycle at 30°C or below, with mild soap. No bleach.
3. **Don't wring it.** Press the water out gently, especially around the crest.
4. **Dry in the shade.** Strong sun fades colours fast and cracks prints. Hang it on a hanger, not a line peg.
5. **No hot iron on prints.** If you must iron, do it inside out on low heat.

> Inside out. Cold water. Shade dry.

White kits need a little extra care. Wash them on their own so they don't pick up colour from other clothes.
