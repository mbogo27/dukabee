A good jersey shouldn't sit in the drawer between games. Here are four easy ways to wear yours through the week.

## With denim

Straight-leg jeans and clean white sneakers. The easiest outfit there is, and it works with almost any kit.

## Under an open shirt

Throw an unbuttoned overshirt or denim jacket over a dark kit like the Chelsea laurel edition. Instant streetwear.

## Match one colour

Pick one colour from the trims and repeat it once, in your cap, socks or sneakers. More than once starts to look like a uniform.

## Size up for a relaxed look

One size up gives that loose streetwear fit. Check our [size guide](/magazine/size-guide) first.
