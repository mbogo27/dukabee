How it works

From shop details to a shop.

    Your shop name, WhatsApp number and products. Soon we'll also read them from your website or Instagram.

  2. We build every page

    Home, shop with filters, and a page for each product, styled in your own brand colours.

  3. Buyers order on WhatsApp

    They add to cart, pick size or colour, and send you a complete order message. You confirm and deliver.
