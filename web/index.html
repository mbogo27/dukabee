How it works

From idea to a live shop.

    Upload your logo, or pick a colour and a style. No logo? We have a clean default.

  2. Add your details and products

    Your WhatsApp number, location and delivery terms, plus 3 to 5 products. Start from a ready-made niche and edit anything.

    Browse your live store, search and filter it, try the cart. Like it? Tap "I want this store" and we'll get it live.
